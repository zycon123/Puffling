Delete Orbuff Account & Data

This page is the external account-deletion resource for Orbuff by Zycon Studios. You can request deletion even if you cannot currently use the app.

Fastest method: delete inside Orbuff

  1. Open Orbuff.
  2. Open System & Support.
  3. Choose Delete guest account.
  4. Read the deletion notice and enter the requested confirmation word.
  5. After the server confirms deletion, Orbuff removes the saved guest identity, recovery credential and local account/wallet session data from the device and restarts.

If you cannot access the app

Use the Email deletion request button above or email zyconstudios@protonmail.com with the subject Orbuff Account Deletion Request.

Please include any information you still have that can help identify the correct records, especially your Orbuff Account ID, approximate last activity date, and platform. Do not send your recovery key, passwords, payment-card details, store credentials, or unrelated sensitive information by email.

Because Orbuff guest accounts are pseudonymous and are not necessarily linked to an email address, we may need additional information to identify the correct guest account safely. If a record cannot be reliably matched to your request, we will explain what additional information is needed rather than deleting another player's data.

What is deleted

For a verified guest-account deletion, Orbuff is designed to remove account-linked server records including:

  • ranked profile and competitive account data;
  • server-authoritative Orbuff inventory and inventory migration state;
  • trade records involving the deleted account;
  • boss-session records;
  • server reward and acquisition-grant records;
  • account-linked leaderboard score rows;
  • the account recovery credential record.

The guest identity is revoked so the deleted token and recovery key cannot be used to restore those records. A minimal deletion/revocation record containing the deleted pseudonymous account identifier may be retained to prevent the deleted identity from being reused.

If the account has a linked paid-Diamond wallet, deletion removes the active account-to-wallet link and disables that wallet so existing wallet tokens can no longer spend or receive purchases.

What is not automatically deleted

Local device save data: guest-account deletion does not automatically erase gameplay progress stored only on your device. Local data can be removed separately using Orbuff's local reset option, by clearing app/site storage, or by uninstalling the app where applicable.

Legacy leaderboard entries: older beta leaderboard entries are not always linked to a guest account identifier. If you want an older result removed, include enough information in your email for us to identify it safely.

Purchase/accounting records: real-money purchases are not currently enabled for public release. If they are enabled later, some store transaction, wallet-ledger or reconciliation records may need to be retained after account deletion for duplicate prevention, refunds, fraud prevention, accounting, disputes, or legal obligations. Where practical, those retained records are kept without an active guest-account link.

Processing and confirmation

Deletion requests are reviewed using the information available to identify the correct records. We may contact you at the email address you use for the request if clarification is needed. Once the relevant guest account has been identified and deletion is completed, the associated deletable server records are permanently removed from active Orbuff systems.
