# Orbuff store listing copy — launch draft

This is ready-to-edit store copy for the first public Orbuff release. Keep claims synchronized with the production build submitted for review.

## English

### App name
Orbuff

### App Store subtitle
Collect. Evolve. Race higher.

### Google Play short description
Jump higher, collect Orbuffs, defeat bosses, evolve your team and race rivals.

### Full description
Meet the Orbuffs — small energy creatures living high above the clouds.

Jump through an endless vertical world, collect new Orbuffs and build a team with different traits and abilities. Push your best height, take on increasingly dangerous bosses, develop your Orbuffs and discover stronger forms as you progress.

Orbuff combines fast vertical jumping with collection, progression and competitive modes:

- Collect a growing Orbdex of unique Orbuffs.
- Develop and evolve your favorites through gameplay.
- Protect and recover Orbuffs through the OrbVault system.
- Challenge bosses and replay defeated bosses in Boss Rush.
- Race a friend or a random rival to 1500 m in Race My Orbuff.
- Use each Orbuff's race ability strategically during multiplayer.
- Complete achievements, earn rewards and chase higher scores.
- Customize your experience with cosmetics and progression upgrades.

Your journey does not end after the bosses. Keep climbing and see how high you can go.

Online features require an internet connection. Some features may use a pseudonymous guest account to protect multiplayer, ranking, inventory and account data. Privacy and account deletion controls are available under System & Support.

### Suggested keywords / themes
orbuff, jumper, collection, creatures, evolution, cloud, arcade, boss, race, multiplayer, endless

## Review-note draft

Orbuff can create a pseudonymous guest account automatically when the reviewer enters online features. No email/password registration is required for the current guest flow. Account deletion is available inside the app under **System & Support → Delete guest account**, and an external deletion-request resource is published at the account deletion URL listed in `STORE_SUBMISSION_METADATA.md`.

Real-money Diamond purchases must not be described as live in store copy until the native billing bridge and provider verification are production-enabled and verified in the submitted build.
