# Puffling Trademark Clearance Review

**Review date:** 12 September 2026  
**Project:** Puffling / Zycon Studios  
**Purpose:** practical pre-launch trademark clearance for a downloadable/mobile/online game.

> This is a documented research review, not a lawyer's formal legal opinion and not a certified register search. Trademark rights are territorial and can also arise from unregistered use in some jurisdictions.

## Executive conclusion

**Do not treat `PUFFLING` as cleared for a worldwide game launch.**

The most serious finding is an exact UK word mark `puffling`, application `UK00004346280`, filed 26 February 2026 by Aaron Costello in Nice Class 9. The published goods cover software, downloadable computer software, mobile software, smartphone software and downloadable applications. Current third-party register data reports the application as registered. Those goods overlap directly with a downloadable/mobile game.

A second material conflict is Disney's older word mark `PUFFLE`. The EU registration `006595326` remains listed active through 21 January 2028 in Class 9 and includes computer software. The UK also has a registered Disney `PUFFLE` mark `UK00002440620` in Class 41 for providing online computer games. `PUFFLE` is not identical to `PUFFLING`, but the marks share the same distinctive `PUFFL-` beginning and the goods/services overlap directly with the Puffling game.

Norway is more favorable: Disney previously owned Norwegian word mark `PUFFLE`, registration `255272`, but Patentstyret's 2020 gazette lists registration 255272 among marks that were not renewed. No exact current Norwegian `PUFFLING` registration was identified in the searches performed for this review. That does **not** amount to certified Norwegian clearance because unregistered/established rights and newer records must still be considered.

## United Kingdom — exact conflict

### `puffling` — UK00004346280

Official UK Intellectual Property Office Trade Mark Journal 2026/011, published 13 March 2026, records:

- word mark: `puffling`
- application: `UK00004346280`
- filing date: 26 February 2026
- owner/applicant: Aaron Costello
- Nice Class: 9
- goods include software, computer software, software applications, downloadable computer software, downloadable computer software applications, mobile software, software for smartphones, downloadable software applications and downloadable computer software for data management.

This is a direct goods overlap with a downloadable Android/iOS game. Current third-party register data reports this application as **Registered**.

### Disney `PUFFLE` — UK00002440620

Current UK trademark database aggregations report Disney Canada Inc.'s `PUFFLE` as registered in Class 41 for:

> Entertainment services, namely, providing on-line computer games.

This is directly within Puffling's intended service area.

### UK conclusion

Launching a downloadable/mobile/online game under the exact primary name `Puffling` in the UK has an **unacceptably high clearance risk without specialist legal advice, consent/licensing, successful challenge to the earlier right, or a rebrand**.

Adding a subtitle or publisher prefix does not automatically solve the conflict if `Puffling` remains the dominant element shown to customers.

## European Union — Disney `PUFFLE`

### EUTM 006595326

Current EU register-derived data shows:

- mark: `PUFFLE`
- type: word mark
- EUIPO file number: `006595326`
- filed: 21 January 2008
- registered: 20 April 2011
- expiry shown: 21 January 2028
- Nice Classes: 9 and 18
- owner reported: Disney Canada Inc.
- Class 9 includes computer hardware, **computer software** and computer peripherals.

The mark is not identical to `PUFFLING`, but the comparison is significant because:

1. both begin with `PUFFL-`;
2. they are visually and phonetically close;
3. the relevant goods are software/computer software;
4. Puffling is intended as a game/entertainment product, close to Disney's historical Puffle/Club Penguin field.

The existence of an old registration does not by itself answer every enforceability question. In the EU, long-standing registrations can be subject to genuine-use requirements in relevant disputes. This review did **not** determine whether Disney could establish current genuine use for every relevant EU good/service. Treat that as a lawyer-level issue, not as permission to launch.

## Norway

### Historical `PUFFLE` registration 255272

Patentstyret's official 2010 trademark gazette records:

- word mark: `PUFFLE`
- registration no.: `255272`
- registration date: 26 April 2010
- owner at registration: Disney Online Studios Canada Inc.
- classes included 9, 16, 18, 20, 25 and 28.

Patentstyret's official trademark gazette no. 46/2020 lists registration `255272` under **merker som ikke er fornyet** (marks not renewed). The old Norwegian registration therefore should not be treated as a currently renewed registration.

No exact current Norwegian `PUFFLING` registration was identified in this research.

### Why Norway is still not automatically clear

Patentstyret explains that confusion is assessed from similarity of the signs and similarity of the goods/services, including visual appearance, pronunciation, associations, purpose, sales channels and competition. Patentstyret also notes that its examination does not discover every company name, business identifier or unregistered established trademark; holders of such rights may still object.

**Recommended Norwegian next step:** order Patentstyret's paid **full registrerbarhetsvurdering** for the intended word mark and actual goods/services before filing or investing heavily in the name.

## United States

The research performed did not surface a live exact US federal registration for the word `PUFFLING` covering game/software goods.

However, there is active commercial use of the exact name:

- App Store listing: `Puffling: Early Childhood Play`
- seller: Andrew Cavanagh
- copyright: `© 2026 Puffling AI Inc.`
- category: Education
- in-app purchases are offered.

Puffling AI's public materials state the business was founded in 2025 and the app launched in 2026.

US rights can arise from use as well as federal registration. The exact scope, geographic priority and service overlap of Puffling AI's rights were not determined by this review. Consequently, the absence of a surfaced exact federal registration should **not** be treated as a green light.

Historical US `PUFFLE` records connected to Disney appear abandoned/cancelled in the sources reviewed, which improves the picture compared with the EU/UK but does not eliminate the current Puffling AI issue.

## Canada

The Canadian Trademarks Database shows Disney/New Horizon Interactive's historical `PUFFLE` application `1297437` as abandoned. The active `Puffling: Early Childhood Play` app is nevertheless available in the Canadian App Store. This produces commercial-name risk even though the historical Disney Canadian filing identified in the review is dead.

## Existing market use and distinctiveness

`puffling` is also an ordinary English term for a young puffin. The word appears in books, wildlife media and other commercial names. This can affect how broad an exclusive claim to the word might be, depending on the goods/services and jurisdiction.

At the same time, the game/software field is the key problem here: the UK has an exact Class 9 `puffling` right, while Disney has `PUFFLE` rights in software and online games in important territories.

## Intended classes for Puffling game

A filing strategy should be built from the actual release plan. The likely core classes to review are:

- **Class 9:** downloadable computer game software, downloadable mobile game software, downloadable applications;
- **Class 41:** entertainment services, providing online computer/video games;
- **Class 28:** toys, plush toys, physical games if Puffling merchandise is planned;
- **Class 25:** clothing if branded apparel is planned;
- potentially Class 16 for printed goods and other classes only where actual goods/services justify them.

Do not file an unnecessarily broad specification simply to occupy classes; use wording matched to real or genuinely intended commerce.

## Recommended decision

### Preferred route — rename before store launch

The lowest-risk commercial route is to choose a new, invented primary brand **before** App Store/Google Play packaging, paid marketing, domains, merchandise and trademark filing. Then run the same clearance process against the new candidate before committing to it.

For the replacement name, require:

1. no exact/confusingly similar active marks in Class 9/41 in Norway, EU, UK and US;
2. no material app-store use by another software/game company;
3. usable domain/social handles where commercially important;
4. enough inherent distinctiveness to register as a word mark;
5. no obvious collision with famous game/character brands.

### If retaining `Puffling`

Do **not** proceed as though it is globally cleared. Before launch:

1. obtain a full Patentstyret registrability/confusion preliminary assessment for Norway;
2. obtain a UK trademark-law opinion on UK00004346280 and UK00002440620;
3. obtain an EU opinion on EUTM 006595326, including any genuine-use/non-use implications;
4. investigate Puffling AI Inc.'s US filings, first-use evidence and territorial rights through a US trademark professional;
5. consider consent/coexistence/licensing only if commercially realistic;
6. geo-restrict launch territories if counsel concludes some territories are unsafe.
