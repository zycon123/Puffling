Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Orbuff, developed by Zycon Studios, handles information when you play the game, use online features, contact support, or use account and purchase-related services.

Account & data deletionPrivacy contact

1. Information Orbuff handles

Local game data

Orbuff stores gameplay information on your device, such as language and audio settings, progression, owned and discovered Orbuffs, OrbVault state, rewards, scores, cooldowns, and other save data needed for the game to function.

Guest account and online identity

When you use online features, Orbuff may create a pseudonymous guest account. The service uses a randomly generated account identifier and authentication token to protect multiplayer, ranking, inventory, trade, boss-session, and reward systems.

Leaderboard information

If you submit a leaderboard result, Orbuff may process the display name you chose, score or height, a run-validation signature, game version, and submission time.

Multiplayer and gameplay activity

Online Race, ranked, trade, boss and reward systems may process gameplay state needed to match players, validate results, protect inventory, settle outcomes, prevent duplicate rewards, and reduce cheating or abuse.

Virtual currency and purchases

Orbuff contains a Diamond wallet and purchase-verification design. If real-money purchases are enabled, transaction identifiers, platform, product identifier, entitlement or verification status, and related reconciliation data may be processed. Payment-card credentials are handled by the applicable app-store payment provider rather than by Orbuff.

2. How information is used

  • Provide, save, recover, and operate game features.
  • Authenticate guest accounts and secure online sessions.
  • Operate multiplayer, rankings, leaderboards, inventory, trades, boss rewards, and virtual currency.
  • Prevent cheating, fraud, duplicate rewards, invalid purchases, and technical abuse.
  • Troubleshoot problems and respond to support or privacy requests.
  • Meet legal, store-platform, security, and accounting obligations where applicable.

3. Service providers and sharing

Orbuff may use infrastructure providers to host game services and public web pages. App-store providers such as Apple or Google may process purchase and distribution information when their services are used. Orbuff does not currently include an advertising SDK or a dedicated behavioral analytics SDK.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect users or the service, or to investigate security, fraud, or abuse.

4. Retention

Local save data remains on your device until it is reset, removed by the operating system, or the app/browser storage is cleared. Server-side records are retained for as long as reasonably needed to provide the related feature, maintain security and integrity, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

Leaderboard results are presented using a rolling recent-results window, and the service is designed to periodically remove older score rows. Deleted guest account identifiers may be retained in a minimal deletion/revocation record so a deleted identity cannot later be reused to restore removed online data.

If paid purchases are enabled, some transaction or reconciliation records may need to be retained for fraud prevention, accounting, refund handling, or legal obligations even after other account-linked data is deleted. Any such retention will be limited to what is required for those purposes.

5. Account and data deletion

Guest-account deletion is available inside Orbuff under System & Support. The in-app flow permanently removes account-linked ranked profile data, authoritative Orbuff inventory, trade records involving that account, boss-session records, and server reward/acquisition records. It also revokes the deleted guest identity.

Deleting the server guest account does not automatically erase local game progress stored only on your device. You can remove local data separately by using the app's local reset option, clearing app/site storage, or uninstalling the app where applicable.

You can also request deletion without using the app through the public Orbuff Account & Data Deletion page.

6. Security

Orbuff uses signed authentication tokens for protected online systems and server-side validation for security-sensitive multiplayer, inventory, reward, and purchase flows. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, but reasonable safeguards are used to reduce unauthorized access and manipulation.

7. Children and guardians

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided information that should be reviewed or deleted, contact Zycon Studios using the address below. We will handle the request in accordance with applicable law and the information available to identify the relevant records.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws may give you rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or other handling of personal information. You may contact us to make a privacy request. We may need reasonable information to verify the request and identify the relevant records.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Orbuff's features, service providers, legal obligations, or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are published.
