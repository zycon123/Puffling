Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Orbuff, developed by Zycon Studios, handles information when you play the game, use online features, contact support, recover or delete a guest account, or use purchase-related services.

1. Information Orbuff handles

Local game data

Orbuff stores gameplay information on your device, such as language and audio settings, progression, owned and discovered Orbuffs, OrbVault state, rewards, scores, cooldowns, your local player name, and other save data needed for the game to function.

Guest account and account recovery

When you use protected online features, Orbuff may create a pseudonymous guest account. The service uses a randomly generated account identifier and authentication token to protect multiplayer, ranking, inventory, trade, boss-session, reward, leaderboard, and account-linked wallet systems.

Orbuff can also generate a recovery key so you can restore the same guest account after reinstalling the app or changing device. The plaintext recovery key is kept by you/on your device; the Orbuff server stores only a SHA-256 hash of the recovery key. Recovery keys can be replaced, and a deleted account cannot be recovered.

Leaderboard information

If you submit a leaderboard result, Orbuff may process the score or height, a run-validation signature, game version, submission time, and—when authenticated—the pseudonymous guest account identifier. Your player-entered local display name is not submitted for public leaderboard presentation. Public leaderboard entries use a server-generated alias such as Orbuff-XXXXXX.

Multiplayer and gameplay activity

Online Race, ranked, trade, boss and reward systems may process gameplay state needed to match players, validate results, protect inventory, settle outcomes, prevent duplicate rewards, and reduce cheating or abuse.

Virtual currency and purchases

Orbuff contains a paid-Diamond wallet and purchase-verification system. A recoverable guest account can be linked to the same server-side wallet so the authoritative paid-Diamond balance can be restored after account recovery. If real-money purchases are enabled, transaction identifiers, platform, product identifier, entitlement or verification status, wallet balance/ledger information, and related reconciliation data may be processed. Payment-card credentials are handled by the applicable app-store payment provider rather than by Orbuff.

2. How information is used

  • Provide, save, recover, and operate game features.
  • Authenticate guest accounts, restore them after reinstall/device change, and secure online sessions.
  • Operate multiplayer, rankings, leaderboards, inventory, trades, boss rewards, and virtual currency.
  • Verify purchases and restore the correct paid-Diamond wallet to a recovered account.
  • Prevent cheating, fraud, duplicate rewards, invalid purchases, and technical abuse.
  • Troubleshoot problems and respond to support or privacy requests.
  • Meet legal, store-platform, security, and accounting obligations where applicable.

3. Service providers and sharing

Orbuff may use infrastructure providers to host game services and public web pages. App-store providers such as Apple or Google may process purchase and distribution information when their services are used. Orbuff does not currently include an advertising SDK or a dedicated behavioral analytics SDK.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect users or the service, or to investigate security, fraud, or abuse.

4. Retention

Local save data remains on your device until it is reset, removed by the operating system, or the app/browser storage is cleared. Server-side records are retained for as long as reasonably needed to provide the related feature, maintain security and integrity, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

Leaderboard results are presented using a rolling recent-results window, and the service is designed to periodically remove older score rows. Legacy beta leaderboard names are anonymized rather than publicly displayed. Deleted guest account identifiers may be retained in a minimal deletion/revocation record so a deleted identity cannot later be reused to restore removed online data.

If paid purchases are enabled, some store transaction, wallet-ledger, or reconciliation records may need to be retained after account deletion for duplicate prevention, refunds, fraud prevention, accounting, dispute handling, or legal obligations. Where practical, the active guest-account link is removed and the wallet is disabled when the guest account is deleted.

5. Account recovery and deletion

Account backup/recovery controls and guest-account deletion are available inside Orbuff under System & Support. Keep your recovery key private: anyone with both your Account ID and valid recovery key may be able to restore that pseudonymous account.

The in-app deletion flow permanently removes account-linked ranked profile data, authoritative Orbuff inventory, trade records involving that account, boss-session records, server reward/acquisition records, account-linked leaderboard scores, and the account recovery credential. It also revokes the deleted guest identity.

If a paid-Diamond wallet is linked to that account, deletion removes the active account-to-wallet link and disables the wallet so previously issued wallet tokens can no longer spend or receive purchases. Store transaction or ledger records may still be retained where necessary for the purposes described in the Retention section.

Deleting the server guest account does not automatically erase local game progress stored only on your device. You can remove local data separately by using the app's local reset option, clearing app/site storage, or uninstalling the app where applicable.

You can also request deletion without using the app through the public Orbuff Account & Data Deletion page.

6. Security

Orbuff uses signed authentication tokens for protected online systems, hashed recovery credentials, and server-side validation for security-sensitive multiplayer, inventory, reward, wallet, and purchase flows. Recovery keys and store credentials should never be shared with untrusted parties. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, but reasonable safeguards are used to reduce unauthorized access and manipulation.
